Who Are We?

About Us: Overview

Navjivan Valves is a supplier of high-quality valves, pipes, flanges and fittings from various top brands, catering to diverse industries across India. With a steadfast commitment to excellence and reliability, we take pride in delivering quality products and dependable service to our customers.

Founded on principles of integrity, reliability and customer satisfaction, Navjivan Valves has grown into a trusted name in the valve supply industry. Our product range includes gate valves, globe valves, ball valves, butterfly valves and control valves, designed to suit applications across oil & gas, petrochemical, power generation, water treatment and more.

In addition to valves, we offer pipes, flanges and fittings, sourced and supplied to ensure seamless connections and dependable performance in industrial piping systems. Our partnerships with established manufacturing brands allow us to provide a comprehensive product range that meets varied customer needs.

We are dedicated to building strong relationships with our clients — understanding their requirements and providing the right product for the job, backed by prompt communication and support.
