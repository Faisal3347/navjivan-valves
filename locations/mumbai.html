Navjivan Valves supplies industrial valves, flanges, pipes and fittings to manufacturing plants, EPC contractors and process industries across the Mumbai metropolitan region. Our office is based in Thane, within easy reach of Mumbai's industrial corridors, ports and process facilities.

Mumbai and its surrounding industrial belt are home to refineries, chemical plants, power stations, pharmaceutical manufacturers and a dense network of engineering and fabrication businesses. Navjivan Valves supports these industries with a wide product range — gate, globe, ball, butterfly, check, plug and safety valves, alongside flanges, pipes and fittings sourced from trusted Indian manufacturers including Audco, Leader, Kirloskar, KSB, Spirax/Forbes Marshall, Uniklinger and Unitek. Whether you need a single valve for a maintenance shutdown or a bulk order for a new project, our team can advise on the right product, prepare a quotation and support delivery to sites across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and the wider metropolitan region.
