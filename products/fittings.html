Types

Types of Fittings We Supply

Elbows

Used to change the direction of a pipe run, typically at 45° or 90°, in a range of radii to suit the installation.

Tee & Reducing Tee

Used to branch a pipeline into two or more directions, with reducing tees accommodating a smaller branch outlet.

Reducers

Connect two pipe sections of different diameters, available in concentric and eccentric configurations.

Laterals

Provide an angled branch connection, commonly used where flow direction needs to be maintained through the branch.

U-Bends

Redirect flow through 180°, commonly used in heat exchanger and process piping layouts.

End Caps

Used to close off the end of a pipe run or branch.

Stub Ends

Used with lap joint flanges to create a rotatable flanged connection, useful for alignment and reducing flange material costs.
