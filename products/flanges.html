Types

Types of Flanges We Supply

Alloy Steel Flanges

Alloy steel flanges combine high strength with good corrosion and temperature resistance, suited to demanding high-pressure, high-temperature piping in refineries, power plants and petrochemical service.

Carbon Steel Flanges

Carbon steel flanges are a cost-effective, widely used choice for general industrial piping across oil & gas, power and process applications.

Stainless Steel Flanges

Stainless steel flanges offer excellent corrosion resistance, making them well suited to chemical, pharmaceutical, food processing and marine piping systems.

Forged Flanges

Forged flanges are manufactured under high pressure and temperature for superior mechanical strength, used across high-pressure piping systems and critical process lines.

Mild Steel Flanges

Mild steel flanges provide an economical option for lower-pressure, general-purpose piping applications.

Plate Flanges

Plate (slip-on/blind style) flanges are machined from flat plate stock, commonly used for lower-pressure connections and blind-end closures.

Welding Neck Flanges

Welding neck flanges feature a tapered hub that is butt-welded to the pipe, providing excellent strength and stress distribution for high-pressure piping.

Slip-On Flanges

Slip-on flanges slide over the pipe end and are fillet-welded in place, offering an easier-to-align, economical option for lower-pressure applications.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which flange type should I choose?
The right flange type depends on the pressure rating, pipe wall thickness, service temperature and installation method — welding neck and forged flanges suit higher-pressure lines, while slip-on and plate flanges are more economical for lower-pressure applications. Our team can help you specify the right type for your project.
What materials are flanges available in?
Navjivan Valves supplies flanges in alloy steel, carbon steel, stainless steel, forged steel and mild steel, selected according to the process fluid, pressure and corrosion requirements.
Do you supply flanges to match ASME/ANSI standards?
Yes — our flanges are supplied to match standard piping specifications used across oil & gas, power and process industries. Share your project specification and we will confirm exact standards and availability.
