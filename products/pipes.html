Types

Types of Pipes We Supply

Seamless Pipes

Manufactured without a welded seam, seamless pipes offer high strength and uniformity, suited to high-pressure oil & gas and process piping.

ERW Pipes

Electric Resistance Welded (ERW) pipes are formed and welded along a straight seam, providing a cost-effective option for a wide range of pressure and structural applications.

Spiral Welded Pipes

Manufactured by spiral-welding steel coil, these pipes are commonly used for large-diameter water, slurry and structural pipeline applications.

Square & Rectangular Pipes

Used in structural and general engineering applications where a non-round cross-section is required.

Hydraulic Pipes

Precision pipes built to withstand the high pressures found in hydraulic power and fluid control systems.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between seamless and ERW pipes?
Seamless pipes are extruded without a welded seam, giving high strength and uniformity for demanding pressure applications. ERW (Electric Resistance Welded) pipes are formed and welded along a straight seam, offering a cost-effective option for a wide range of pressure and structural uses.
What sizes and materials are available?
Pipe sizes, wall thickness and material grades are supplied per project requirement — share your specification with our team for a prompt quotation.
