Industrial Valve Supplier for the Power Generation Industry

  • Power Generation

Power generation applications — from boiler feedwater and main steam lines to cooling water and condensate systems — demand valves capable of sustained high-pressure, high-temperature service. Navjivan Valves supplies forged steel gate and globe valves, PRDS systems, and safety relief valves for thermal and process power plant applications.

How Navjivan Valves Supports Power Generation

  • Pressure-sealed and bellow-sealed globe valves for main steam and high-pressure lines
  • PRDS systems for pressure reduction and desuperheating between steam headers
  • Forbes Marshall safety relief valves (FMSRV) for boiler and pressure vessel protection
  • Butterfly and gate valves for cooling water and lower-pressure utility systems
  • Check valves for boiler feed pump discharge protection
